Security engineering — running in production

Cyber-protecting a fleet of 20+ solar installations

Production

OT / ICS security · Burkina Faso, administered from Mainz

ProblemEvery installation arrives with its inverters, battery management and telemetry on one flat network, behind whatever router the ISP supplied — the same segment as household devices. At this count, hardening each site by improvisation does not scale, and I administer all of them from Germany, roughly 4,000 km away, with only local technicians on the ground. BuiltOne hardened reference design, replicated site by site. A MikroTik router takes over routing and firewalling and the ISP box drops to a plain modem. VLAN segmentation isolates the OT plane — GoodWe inverters, BYD storage, SEMS+ telemetry — from the general LAN, so monitoring reaches the plant while the plant shares a broadcast domain with nothing. Impact20+ installations under one reviewable baseline. Bringing a new site online is a known procedure rather than a fresh decision, and morning grid-transfer faults get diagnosed from telemetry instead of a phone call.
